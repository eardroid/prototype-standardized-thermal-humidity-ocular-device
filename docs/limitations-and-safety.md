# Limitations and safety

This repository describes a bench-test proof of concept only.

- The DHT11 measures surrounding air, not an eye-facing surface.
- The software threshold is an interlock for demonstration, not a validated safe temperature.
- The first atomizer photographed here was not working; the mist path is an intended architecture, not a demonstrated clinical function.
- The Peltier needs a suitable external supply, current limit, heatsink, and validated switching stage.
- The breadboard wiring and taped enclosure are temporary prototype construction.
- Moisture must be kept away from the electronics, and condensation must be managed.
- No clinical, biocompatibility, electrical-safety, or regulatory evaluation has been performed.

Before any future physical-contact study, the design would need independent thermal protection, redundant sensing, fusing, secure insulation, a controlled test protocol, and qualified engineering and medical review. Do not use this prototype on or near a person’s eyes.
